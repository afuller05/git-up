# Voice Clone Recording Script — N2 Report Host (Andy)

Purpose: record clean audio of the host's real voice for an ElevenLabs
**Professional Voice Clone** (included in the Creator plan).

- Instant Voice Clone: 1–2 minutes of audio (quick, less accurate).
- Professional Voice Clone: 30+ minutes (1–3 hours ideal). Read this script
  several times, on different days, in the same room with the same mic.

## Recording tips

- Quiet, soft room (carpet/curtains), no echo, no background noise or music.
- Same microphone and distance every time (~15–20 cm); headset/USB mic if available.
- Read in your natural hosting voice — warm and conversational, not a "reading" voice.
- Mistakes are fine: pause, then redo the sentence.
- Keep energy consistent. Save as WAV or MP3 at 192 kbps or better.

## Script (~5–6 minutes per read-through)

### Part 1 — Welcome (friendly, upbeat)

Hi everyone, and welcome to the N2 Report! I'm Andy, and every month I get to share
what's happening across our communities. We've got new faces on the team, some great
updates from our properties, and of course, another episode of What Would Andy Do.
So grab a coffee, get comfortable, and let's get into it.

### Part 2 — Explaining a process (clear, steady)

Let's talk about how a work order moves through the system. First, the tenant calls the
Client Service Centre or submits a request online. Next, the request is reviewed and
assigned a priority — emergency, urgent, or routine. An emergency, like a flood or no heat
in January, gets a response the same day. Routine requests are scheduled, and the tenant
gets a call to confirm the date. Once the work is done, the technician closes the order,
and we follow up to make sure everything was fixed properly.

### Part 3 — Numbers, dates and places

This quarter, we completed four thousand, two hundred and seventy-five work orders.
Our average response time dropped from nine days to six and a half. On Tuesday,
October the thirteenth, at ten thirty a.m., we'll be hosting a community barbecue at
1255 Donald Street. The unit turnover target is twenty-one days — and last month we hit
eighteen. Communities in the east end include Vanier, Overbrook and Lowertown, and out
west, Carlington, Britannia and Bayshore.

### Part 4 — Warm and caring

I know that when something breaks in your home, it's stressful. You want to know
someone's listening, and that it's going to get fixed. That's what our team is here for.
Every person who calls us deserves to be treated with respect and patience — and that's
something I'm really proud of.

### Part 5 — Serious safety message (slower, firm)

Now, a quick but important reminder. Never use a barbecue or propane heater indoors or
on a covered balcony. Test your smoke alarm every month. And if you ever smell gas, leave
the building right away and call 911 from outside. These steps save lives.

### Part 6 — Conversational and playful

So… what would Andy do? Well — honestly? The first thing I'd do is not panic. Then I'd
grab the right tool for the job. Right? It's amazing how often the simplest fix is the
right one. And if you're not sure, ask! Seriously — no question is a silly question.
Okay, maybe one or two. But still!

### Part 7 — Questions and lists

Did you know we manage more than fifteen thousand homes? Have you met our new
coordinator yet? Here's what you'll need for your inspection: your lease, a list of any
repairs, and access to every room — including closets and the storage locker.

### Part 8 — Sign-off

That's it for this month's N2 Report. Thanks for watching, thanks for everything you do
for our tenants, and I'll see you next month. Take care, everyone!
